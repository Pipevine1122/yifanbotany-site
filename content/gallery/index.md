## Studio Viola™

Other than research, I also love appreciating the beauty behind organisms.

**Studio Viola™**, est. 2025, founded by me, Ms. Binyu Zhao, and Ms. Yushan Cai, pursues the goal of making botanical and biological knowledge accessible yet deeply engaging through beautifully crafted, clearly representational visual aids with the aim of wider reach and greater impact. Here, we create, illustrate, and digitize art inspired by plants, animals, fungi, and the natural world. We work with old-school botanical techniques such as pointillism and ink drawing, and we also embrace modern approaches including digital painting and 3D modeling. Please enjoy some of our featured works below.
